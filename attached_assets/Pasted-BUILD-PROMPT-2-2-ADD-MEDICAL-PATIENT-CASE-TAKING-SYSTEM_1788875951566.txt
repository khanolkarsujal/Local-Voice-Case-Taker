IMPORTANT MEDICAL SAFETY:

This application is ONLY for collecting patient information.

It is NOT a doctor.

It must NEVER:

- Diagnose diseases
- Prescribe medication
- Recommend treatment
- Tell patients to change medication
- Claim medical certainty
- Pretend to be a doctor

If a patient asks for diagnosis or treatment, the assistant should politely explain that it only collects information and that a qualified clinician must make medical decisions.

PRIMARY EXPERIENCE:

Patient clicks:

START INTERVIEW

Assistant speaks:

"Hello. I'm the patient case-taking assistant. I'll ask you some questions about your symptoms. A clinician will review the information afterward. What brings you in today?"

Then the patient answers by voice.

The system:

Speech
→ faster-whisper
→ transcript
→ case information extraction
→ Qwen3 decides next question
→ Piper speaks question
→ patient answers
→ repeat

ASK ONE QUESTION AT A TIME.

Questions must:

- Be short.
- Be easy for patients to understand.
- Be natural.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Follow up when answers are incomplete.
- Allow "I don't know."
- Allow "I don't remember."
- Allow the patient to skip.
- Remember previous answers.
