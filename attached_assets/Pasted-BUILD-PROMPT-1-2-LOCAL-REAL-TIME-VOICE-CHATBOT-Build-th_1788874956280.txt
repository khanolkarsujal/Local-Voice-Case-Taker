VOICE EXPERIENCE:

User clicks:

START INTERVIEW

Then:

1. Browser requests microphone permission.
2. User speaks.
3. Audio is recorded.
4. Audio is sent to FastAPI.
5. Faster-whisper transcribes it.
6. Transcript is displayed.
7. Transcript is sent to Ollama.
8. Ollama generates a response.
9. Response is sent to Piper.
10. Piper returns WAV.
11. Browser automatically plays WAV.
12. Conversation continues.
